THE PROBLEM

Finding the right cancer target
shouldn't take years.

CAR-T therapy rewires a patient's immune cells to kill cancer. It works — but picking the wrong target protein can be fatal. Today, that decision relies on fragmented data, scattered tools, and months of manual research.

01

THE SOLUTION

CARVanta: one platform,
every answer.

CARVanta replaces the entire fragmented workflow with a single AI-powered platform. Score any antigen. Simulate any patient. Discover novel targets. Match to clinical trials.

1

AI Antigen Scoring

8-feature adaptive weighted scoring + Random Forest + XGBoost ensemble trained on 119,816 biomarkers from TCGA, GTEx, UniProt, and HPA. Scores 16,000+ targets across 18 cancers.

2

Patient Digital Twin

Full patient simulator — models T-cell expansion, tumor regression, cytokine storm risk, and treatment outcomes over 12 months. 18 specialized engines including IPI/NCCN-IPI clinical scoring.

3

Real-Time Genomic Analyzer

Upload VCF/FASTA/BAM files. 14 engines: variant calling, neoantigen prediction, HLA typing, TMB/MSI, fusion detection, clonal evolution, pharmacogenomics. Publication-ready figures.

4

Drug Discovery Engine

Graph Neural Networks scan 20,000+ human proteins for novel targets. Includes scFv antibody designer, CAR construct architect, and off-target toxicity prediction.

5

Clinical Trial Matcher

Syncs with ClinicalTrials.gov. AI-powered patient-trial matching, eligibility screening, geographic proximity, biomarker correlation, and outcome prediction.
